Privacy policy — CozyGrams

Last updated 2026-09-28

CozyGrams (package com.cozygrams.tv) is a nonogram puzzle game for Android TV. It has no network permission and collects and shares nothing. There are no accounts, ads, or analytics.

Data stored on your TV

Your puzzle progress, Story Book, and settings are saved in app-private storage on the TV.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions.

Questions about this policy: brad@bradflaugher.com
