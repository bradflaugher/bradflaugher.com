privacy 'Bout That Action

Last updated 2026-10-05

'Bout That Action (package com.bradflaugher.aboutthataction) is an Android game. It collects nothing.

What the app does not do

  • No network permission — the app cannot access the internet at all.
  • No accounts, analytics, ads, crash reports, or tracking of any kind.
  • No online leaderboards, and it never asks you for a rating.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (sound, difficulty, controls, text size, hero), your records (best score, deepest floor, runs, challenges cleared and your best tries) and which tutorial tips you've already seen are stored in app-private storage on your device. They are not backed up to the cloud. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Links out

Share hands the game's Google Play link to your device's share sheet. Rate opens the game's page in the Play Store app, or in your browser. Feedback and Privacy policy open a web page in your browser. Those are other apps, and their own privacy policies apply from there; the game itself sends nothing.

Permissions

The only permission the app requests is vibration, used solely for haptics.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions about this policy: brad@bradflaugher.com
