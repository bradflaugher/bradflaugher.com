Privacy policy — 'Bout That Action

Last updated 2026-09-28

'Bout That Action (package com.bradflaugher.aboutthataction) is an Android game. It collects nothing.

What the app does not do

  • No network permission — the app cannot access the internet at all.
  • No accounts, analytics, ads, crash reports, or tracking of any kind.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (sound, difficulty, controls, hero) and your records (best score, deepest floor, runs) are stored in app-private storage on your device. They are not backed up to the cloud. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Permissions

The only permission the app requests is vibration, used solely for haptics.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions about this policy: brad@bradflaugher.com
