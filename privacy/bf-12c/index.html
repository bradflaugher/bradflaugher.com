Privacy policy — bf-12c

Last updated 2026-10-05

bf-12c (package com.bradflaugher.bf12c) is an RPN calculator for Android. It has no network permission and requests no permissions. It collects and shares nothing.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting, or tracking of any kind.
  • No data ever leaves your device unless you choose to send it, as described under Links out.
  • It never asks you for a rating.

Data stored on your device

Your calculator state, programs, settings, and whether you've seen the first-run tips stay in app-private storage on the device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Links out

The ON menu's Share app hands the app's Google Play link to your device's share sheet, and Rate on Google Play opens the app's page in the Play Store app, or in your browser. Send feedback opens a new GitHub issue in your browser, pre-filled with the app version, your Android version, and your device model so a bug report is useful. Nothing is sent unless you submit that issue yourself, and you can edit or remove those details first. Those are other apps and sites, and their own privacy policies apply.

Contact

Questions about this policy: brad@bradflaugher.com
